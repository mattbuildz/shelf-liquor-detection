Notes:

- Ultralytics prints its own mAP50 during validation. It is only a cross-check, the table uses the `evaluate_map.py` number.
- `cache="ram"` can make runs slightly non-deterministic, so differences below about 0.01 are noise.
- The scoring itself is repeatable. Scoring the same weights again gave identical numbers (A2 on 6 and 9 October, K640 and A3 twice on 9 October), so differences between rows come from training.
- The gap between `evaluate_map.py` and Ultralytics' own mAP50 is not constant (A about 7%, A2 8.8%, K640 6.8%, A3 5.7%, B 4.7%, B30 2.9%, A3-130 6.1%), so one number cannot be converted into the other. Up to B the differences between runs agreed in both (A3 minus K640: +0.080 here, +0.079 in Ultralytics. B minus A3: +0.125 in both). A3-130 against B30 is the first pair where they disagree on the order: B30 is 0.019 higher here and 0.002 lower in Ultralytics. The likely cause of the gap is in `OBSERVATIONS.md`. It predicts that a model less sure of the class gains more in the Ultralytics number, which fits the larger gap of A3-130. Not tested.
- Times are training only, with the per-epoch validation, as written to `results.csv`. Times on the Mac and on Kaggle are different hardware and do not compare as a cost of the change.
